# Changelog

## 2.8.0-beta

- 3x3 mining hammers and area shovels for copper, iron, thorium, and cobalt.
- Tiered mining workbenches and direct tool upgrades.
- Resonance calibration, modifier installation, recycling, repair, and tooltips.
- Survival and Creative safeguards for drops, durability, protection, and full inventories.
- German and English in-game help text.
- Public source release with regression tests and asset validation.

Known limitation: icons and some 3D models are still being refined. Please use
the issue tracker for visual defects and include a screenshot plus the affected
item ID.
